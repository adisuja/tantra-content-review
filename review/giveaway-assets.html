Rules for using the library

  • One post offers one asset. The post's angle, hook and format are new for that profile; the asset need not be.
  • A hero system's page links its single pieces, so one comment can lead into the whole system.
  • Never offer the webinar bonuses themselves (The $1MN+ B2B RevOps Playbook, The $1MN+ AI Funnel Prompt Library, The AI RevOps Revenue Calculator) or the Tantra RevOps OS upsell as a giveaway (BLK-11, BLK-12).
