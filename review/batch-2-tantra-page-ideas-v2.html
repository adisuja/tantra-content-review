Carousels

T1. Your Funnel Leaks Between Marketing and Sales. Here's the Old Way vs the New Way.

  • Hook: Marketing counts leads. Sales counts deals. Nobody owns the path between them, and that's where the money leaks.
  • Teaches:
  • RevOps as one system with one owner and one set of numbers across 7 steps: Stranger, Attention, Lead, Nurtured, Qualified, Buyer, Paid.
  • Then six old-way vs new-way pairs: who owns the path, the numbers, follow-up, who does the work, the tools, and when it breaks.
  • Workshop tie: Day 1 starts here.
  • Stage: TOFU · Sources: WS-01, WS-02
  • Design: Harry Dry #26, "rigid two-row before/after grid, clean cards, generous margins". Runner-up: Harry Dry #21

T2. Webinar, Challenge or Reverse Squeeze? Pick by What Your Buyer Already Believes.

  • Hook: Most teams pick a funnel because they saw it work for someone else. Pick it by what your buyer already believes about you.
  • Summary: One poster per funnel, on a belief scale from "never heard of you" to "knows you":
  • Reverse squeeze: needs 2 or 3 case studies.
  • Challenge: they must try it to believe it.
  • Webinar: one idea reframed; the best first funnel.

Then two more checks: who closes (a person on a call or a page), and the weekly hours each funnel needs. - Workshop tie: Day 1, decision 6. - Stage: TOFU · Sources: WS-04 - Design: Harry Dry #19, "ToFu/MoFu/BoFu three-poster row with hand-drawn spectrum bar". Runner-up: Harry Dry #35

T3. The 13 Messages That Get People to Show Up

  • Hook: A full registration list means nothing if the room is empty. Here are the 13 messages that fill it, and exactly when each goes out.
  • Summary: A stepper with three tracks:
  • Before: confirm, 7 days, the day before at 08:00 their time, 1 hour, 15 minutes, live.
  • Attended: 0, +1, +2, +3 days.
  • No-show: replay, the idea they missed, replay-close day.

One link per message. Late messages are dropped, never sent late. It ends on a table of who gets what. - Workshop tie: Day 2. - Stage: MOFU · Sources: WS-08 - Design: Harry Dry #48, "two-tier stepper grids; final ... comparison table", with a "blue numbered-bar stepper". Runner-up: Austin Belcak #33

T4. Stop, Fix or Scale: The Weekly Channel Check

  • Hook: Three numbers per channel, once a week, decide what you stop, fix or scale. Bounces first: a bouncing inbox burns the domain you warmed.
  • Summary: One identical page per call, coloured by judgement:
  • Red: bounces over 5%, pause today.
  • Orange: click-through under 1%, rewrite the offer.
  • Green: scale at 3% click-through, 1.5% conversion and 2% bounces or less.

The last page: a blank is not a zero. - Workshop tie: Day 3. - Stage: MOFU · Sources: WS-10 - Design: Harry Dry #39, "identical template stamped per slide, color = judgment". Craft: "red/orange/green semantic instant-legibility system". Runner-up: Harry Dry #43

T5. Find the One Step That Leaks Your Funnel

  • Hook: You don't fix a funnel. You fix one step of it. Here's how to find which one, with nothing but division.
  • Summary:
  • Hero object: the funnel.
  • Exhibit 1: each stage rate is one count over the one before.
  • Exhibit 2: divide yours by a reference and take the lowest ratio; a tie goes to the earlier step.
  • Exhibit 3: change one thing, split the next run in half, keep or revert, log it. A new worst step means the loop works.
  • Workshop tie: Day 3.
  • Stage: MOFU · Sources: WS-11, WS-12
  • Design: Harry Dry #45, "hero object → text-emphasis → annotated exhibit → annotated exhibit with radiating marker callouts". Runner-up: Harry Dry #31

T6. Put an AI Agent in Charge of Your Funnel, With Guardrails

  • Hook: Letting an AI agent run your funnel is easy. Stopping it from sending, buying or launching without you is the part nobody shows.
  • Summary: A real tech-setup tutorial, one page per piece, each with a real screenshot of the files:
  • the brief becomes CLAUDE.md
  • context lives in three files (answers, spec, approvals)
  • every call goes through one script that refuses send, launch, buy or delete without your exact words
  • keys stay in .env
  • one fact lives once in the spec (fix the spec, never the tool)
  • one prompt per new session
  • Workshop tie: Day 3.
  • Stage: MOFU/BOFU · Sources: WS-13
  • Design: Austin Belcak #34, "every slide pairs copy with a product UI screenshot". Craft: "annotated real-product screenshots". Runner-up: Patrick James Cumming #42

Single images

T7. One Sentence Every Page, Email and Invite Is Written From

  • Hook: If you can't say your offer in one sentence, your landing page will take five. Here's the sentence, slot by slot.
  • Summary: One colour-coded sentence: "I help {who} go from {before} to {after} using {mechanism}, in {time}, or {guarantee}", with a pill legend for each slot. The rules: the after is a number with a unit and a period, and any tool goes in the mechanism, never the outcome.
  • Workshop tie: Day 1.
  • Stage: TOFU · Sources: WS-05
  • Design: Austin Belcak #7, "color-coded semantic sentence with a pill legend". Runner-up: Alex Vacca #40

T8. Size Your List Backwards From Revenue

  • Hook: Most teams buy a list and hope it's big enough. Start from revenue and divide backwards: four lines tell you how many to invite.
  • Summary: One funnel graphic, one tier per line:
  • buyers = target / price
  • people at the offer = buyers / purchase rate
  • registrants = that / show rate
  • invites = that / registration rate

The left column gives each formula, the right column the rule: only your own rates, and a blank reads "unknown, will measure". - Workshop tie: Day 1. - Stage: TOFU/MOFU · Sources: WS-07 - Design: Alex Vacca #43, "central stacked funnel with left (channel/metrics) and right (execute) annotation columns per tier". Runner-up: Alex Vacca #20

T9. Score Every Application Before Anyone Gets on a Call

  • Hook: Over 70 books a call. 40 to 70 gets a reply within a day. Under 40 goes to nurture. Your calendar should only hold the top band.
  • Summary: A vertical flowchart:
  • the recorded sales presentation, then the application, branching into the three bands
  • each band joins its own follow-up, then everything syncs to the CRM

It teaches qualification as a system, not a gut call. - Workshop tie: Day 2 (reverse squeeze). - Stage: MOFU · Sources: WS-09 - Design: Alex Vacca #26, "single-column pipeline → branches into 3 tier cards → converges to CRM-sync node; dashed loop-back". Runner-up: Alex Vacca #36

T10. Six Stages, Four Layers: The Map of a Revenue Engine

  • Hook: Buying the apps isn't building the engine. Every stage needs four layers: apps, APIs, automations and workflows.
  • Summary: A radial hub: one engine in the centre, 6 stage cards around it (Traffic, Landing and registration, Nurture, Conversion, Revenue and pipeline, the 52-week engine). Each card shows its four layers. Apps alone do nothing.
  • Workshop tie: Day 1 to Day 3.
  • Stage: TOFU · Sources: WS-03
  • Design: Harry Dry #44, "central object with 6 satellite cards, connector lines with dot terminals, bilateral symmetry". Runner-up: Patrick James Cumming #23
